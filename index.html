        Sources & a note on this story

        This is a work of interactive historical fiction. The dates, places, and central events follow published accounts, including Nando Parrado's Miracle in the Andes (2006) and Piers Paul Read's Alive (1974). Some conversations and inner thoughts are dramatized. Where the historical record is uncertain, the story follows the survivors' testimony.

        Further reading: Miracle in the Andes · Alive: The Story of the Andes Survivors · “Uruguayan Air Force Flight 571,” Wikipedia · Society of the Snow (2023).
